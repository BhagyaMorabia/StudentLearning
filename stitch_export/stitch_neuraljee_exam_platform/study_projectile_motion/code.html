Projectile Motion

schedule ~15 min read task_alt 4 PYQ questions

Projectile motion is a form of motion experienced by an object or particle (a projectile) that is projected near the Earth's surface and moves along a curved path under the action of gravity only. This curved path was shown by Galileo to be a parabola.

Key Principles

The key to understanding projectile motion lies in the independence of horizontal and vertical motion. Once the projectile is launched, the only force acting upon it is gravity (assuming air resistance is negligible).

  • Horizontal Motion: Velocity remains constant ($a_x = 0$) because there are no horizontal forces acting on the object.
  • Vertical Motion: Experiences constant acceleration due to gravity ($a_y = -g$).

tips_and_updates Pro Tip for JEE

Always resolve the initial velocity $u$ into horizontal and vertical components immediately: $u_x = u \cos\theta$ and $u_y = u \sin\theta$. Treat these as two entirely separate 1D kinematic problems linked only by time $t$.

Important Formulas

For a projectile launched with initial velocity $u$ at an angle $\theta$ to the horizontal:

1. Equation of Trajectory

$$ y = x \tan\theta - \frac{gx^2}{2u^2 \cos^2\theta} $$

This proves the path is parabolic, as it is a quadratic equation in terms of $x$.

2. Time of Flight ($T$)

$$ T = \frac{2u \sin\theta}{g} $$

3. Maximum Height ($H$)

$$ H = \frac{u^2 \sin^2\theta}{2g} $$

4. Horizontal Range ($R$)

$$ R = \frac{u^2 \sin 2\theta}{g} $$
